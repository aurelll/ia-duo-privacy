2. What IA DUO does

IA DUO Studio is an independent browser extension that coordinates user-initiated collaboration between AI assistants in browser tabs. An optional Windows companion supports local project, file, and conversation-history storage.

3. Information that may be processed

  • Messages, prompts, and responses visible in supported AI conversation interfaces.
  • Website content, URLs, and information about the user's collaboration activity.
  • Preferences, session state, conversation history, diagnostic logs, project data, and user-selected attachments.
  • Personal or sensitive information included in those materials.

4. Transfers between AI services

During collaborations initiated by the user, IA DUO may copy information from one AI conversation to another, such as from ChatGPT to Claude or vice versa. Recipient platforms process content under their own terms and privacy policies. Users should not transfer sensitive or confidential information without appropriate authorization.

5. Local storage and retention

The extension uses browser-local storage, and the Windows companion may use local files and an SQLite database. Retention periods and deletion, backup, recovery, and export procedures must be verified before final release.

6. Network activity and recipients

Besides user-selected AI services, the distribution may access services needed for its operation. Any telemetry, cloud sync, and hosting practices must be audited before claiming they are absent.

7. User controls and rights

Users can stop collaboration sessions and uninstall the extension. Detailed data-access, deletion, and applicable rights procedures will be published following verification.

8. Third-party independence

IA DUO is independent software and is not affiliated with or endorsed by OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, or Microsoft.
